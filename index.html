🔒 Privacy

Privacy Policy

This is a general description of how this app handles information. It is not legal advice, and the ministry should have it reviewed before relying on it.

Stored only on your device

Your prayer journal, notes, favorites, reading progress, settings, saved location, and backups stay in your browser on your own device. They are not sent to us. If you clear your browser data they are lost unless you made a backup.

Your location

If you allow location, it is used only on your device to work out prayer times, Shabbat times, and fast times. It is not sent to our servers. The Torah portion feature asks Hebcal.com for the schedule for a year only. It does not send your location.

Prayer requests by email

The Prayer tab opens your own email app with the request filled in. We receive it only if you press Send. Requests are received at .

Prayer Wall

If you post to the Prayer Wall, the name and request you type are saved on our service (Supabase) and reviewed by an administrator. Approved requests are visible to everyone who opens the app. Tell us if you would like a request removed.

Administrators

Administrators sign in with an email and password through Supabase. Sermons, announcements, and settings that administrators post are stored there.

Giving

The Give button takes you to a payment provider chosen by the ministry. That provider handles your payment details under its own privacy policy. This app never sees or stores your card or bank details.

Other services

Bible text is loaded from getbible.net. Videos on the Sermons tab are played by YouTube, which may set its own cookies. Torah portion dates come from Hebcal.com. The Lexicon loads the public-domain Strong's dictionary from GitHub (or jsDelivr). These services see your device's internet address when your device contacts them.

Children

This app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Questions

To ask about your information or to have something removed, write to .
